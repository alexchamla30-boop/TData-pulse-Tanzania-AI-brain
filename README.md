# 🇹🇿 TDataPulse - Tanzania AI Economy Brain
### A 3-Year Challenge (2026-2029) to Build Tanzania's First Intelligent Financial & Market Data Platform

> **Vision:** Kila Mtanzania awe na mshauri wa kifedha mfukoni - Powered by AI.

---

### 🎯 PROBLEM
Makampuni makubwa Tanzania (Vodacom, NMB, CRDB) na wananchi hawana data ya pamoja ya:
- Bei za DSE / Orbit Securities ETF kwa wakati halisi
- Bei za bidhaa Kariakoo / Mbezi
- Utabiri wa soko

Maamuzi yanafanywa kwa kubahatisha.

### 💡 SOLUTION
TDataPulse ni AI Engine inayounganisha:
1.  **DSE Intelligence:** Scraper ya bei za hisa + ETF ya Orbit (ORBIT-MA-ETF) + Price Prediction Model (LSTM)
2.  **Kariakoo Pulse:** Real-time commodity price tracking
3.  **Swahili Financial Advisor:** Chatbot inayojibu kwa Kiswahili "Niwekeze wapi leo?"
